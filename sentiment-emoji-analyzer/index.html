Architecture & Formulas

Primary Sentiment Score Formula

Calculates the normalized polarity ratio between positive and negative signals over total signal volume:

Sentiment Score = [ (Positive - Negative) / (Positive + Negative + Neutral) ] × 100

• Positive = PosWords + (PosEmojis × 1.5)
• Negative = NegWords + (NegEmojis × 1.5)
• Neutral = NeuWords + NeuEmojis

Word & Modifier Scaling

Each recognized lexical token is scaled by preceding intensifiers, diminishers, and negations:

Word Valence = BaseValence × Modifier
Negation Lookback (3 tokens): val = val × -0.74
Intensifier (1 token): e.g., "very" (×1.30), "extremely" (×1.50)
Diminisher (1-2 tokens): e.g., "slightly" (×0.60), "a bit" (×0.75)

Emoji Repetition & Intensity

Repeated emojis are weighted with diminishing returns, then scaled by the configurable emoji factor:

Repetition Factor = 1 + 0.2 × (count - 1)
Emoji Contribution = Intensity × Repetition × 1.5

Classification & Sarcasm Heuristic

Score thresholds determine the sentiment classification label:

Score ≥ +5 → Positive
Score ≤ -5 → Negative
-5 < Score < +5 → Neutral
Positive Text + Sarcasm Emoji (🙃, 💀) → "Possible Sarcasm"
