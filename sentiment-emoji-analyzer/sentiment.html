How it works

From input to insight

Moodline runs a lexicon-based sentiment engine (VADER-style scoring) alongside a dedicated emoji analyzer, then merges both signals — checking for contradictions along the way — before producing a plain-language summary.

Text / Image input→ OCR extraction→ Emoji detection→ Text sentiment→ Emoji sentiment→ Emotion classification→ Reversal / sarcasm check→ Score + confidence→ Summary
Moodline — a heuristic sentiment demo. Not a substitute for professional linguistic or clinical judgment.
